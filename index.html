Profile

To me, hospitality is rooted in patience, discipline, and genuine care for the person at the table. Over eight years in fine dining and farm-to-table kitchens, I earned my way from hot appetizers to sous chef at Deuxave. I built my foundation on classical technique: whole-animal butchery, handmade pasta, and the steady focus required for dishes like a nine-hour French onion soup. I take pride in leading calm, collaborative kitchens where we respect seasonal ingredients and make every guest feel welcome.
